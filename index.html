Frequently Asked 3 AM Sleep Questions

Quick reassurance for overtired nights.

Why is my baby suddenly inconsolable every evening?

That's the classic "witching hour" (usually 5 PM–10 PM, peaking around weeks 6–8). Newborn nervous systems get overstimulated by the end of the day. Dim the lights, turn on steady brown noise, try a warm bath or stepping outside into cool fresh air for 2 minutes—the sudden change in scenery often hits a gentle reset button.

Is brown noise better than white noise for babies?

Many parents and babies prefer brown noise or pink noise over harsh static white noise because they emphasize deeper, lower frequencies—much closer to the muffled soundscape inside the womb and far more pleasant for parents sleeping in the same room.

When should I stop swaddling my baby?

Stop swaddling as soon as your baby shows any early signs of trying to roll over (often around 8–12 weeks). Transition to a sleeveless sleep sack so their arms are free for safe sleep.

What are red flags that crying isn't just normal fussiness?

Call your pediatrician promptly if crying comes with a fever (especially 100.4°F / 38°C or higher in a baby under 3 months), vomiting, fewer wet diapers than usual, rash, limpness, or high-pitched/painful crying that doesn't resemble their normal cry.
